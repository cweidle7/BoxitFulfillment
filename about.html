EST. 2019 · MILLCREEK, UTAH

We learned fulfillment the hard way, one customs form at a time.

Box It Fulfillment began in 2019 shipping nutraceutical products into markets with strict import rules. Getting the paperwork right wasn't optional, so we built the whole company around it. The goal hasn't changed: make logistics easier, deliver service worth talking about, and help the brands we ship for grow.

01 — THE STORY

From one brand's back office to a cross-border operation

Our first customer was a single supplement brand selling into countries where one mislabeled parcel meant the whole shipment sat at the border. There was no room to wing it. We wrote our receiving, classification, and documentation process around getting it right the first time, and never stopped running it that way.

That's still the difference. Whether a brand ships fifty orders a month or five thousand, the same checks apply and the same team is accountable for them. We'd rather be an extension of your team than the warehouse down the road, the partner who knows where you're headed and helps you get there.

2019

Founded in Millcreek, Utah, fulfilling and exporting nutraceutical orders for one brand.

2020–2021

Word moved through founder networks. We took on e-commerce and DTC brands who needed the customs know-how we had already built.

2022–2023

Expanded the warehouse, added real-time inventory and direct store integrations, and crossed into 40+ destination countries.

Today

A female- and minority-owned 3PL running domestic and cross-border fulfillment at 100% order accuracy and 100% on-time, for brands that want a logistics team, not just a warehouse.
